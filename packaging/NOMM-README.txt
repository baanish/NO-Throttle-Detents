NUCLEAR OPTION DETENTS @VERSION@

Adds hold detents at idle and at the afterburner boundary so the keyboard
throttle does not open the airbrake or light the afterburner by accident.
Nuclear Option Mod Manager installs, updates, enables, disables, and removes
it, and supplies BepInEx.

SUPPORTED CONTROLS

Keyboard and button throttles, with any game setting. Analog throttle axes
(HOTAS levers, sliders, sticks) are not supported and stay vanilla.

BepInEx creates com.baanish.nuclearoption.detents.cfg on first launch.
Settings, supported aircraft, and known limits:
https://github.com/baanish/NO-Throttle-Detents
